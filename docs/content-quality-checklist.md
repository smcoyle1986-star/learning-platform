# Content quality checklist

Use this checklist before publishing a new vocabulary card, image set, theme, game prompt, worksheet, or public lesson resource.

## Vocabulary and images

- The word uses the site's default US English spelling and natural classroom phrasing.
- The image independently communicates the target word; it does not depend on the text to make sense.
- The image shows the intended meaning, grammatical form, and category.
- Any illustration with a Classendo character is appropriate for the target age group and theme.
- The card has an accurate alt text and is not a placeholder unless the lesson intentionally uses a text-only card.

## Teaching accuracy

- The resource suits the stated level, theme, and learning objective.
- Instructions are complete, natural English, and practical for a teacher to follow.
- Example sentences, answers, and game rules have been checked.
- Free and Premium labels match the actual access rules.

## Release check

- Test the item on desktop and mobile.
- For classroom activities and games, test ordinary view, fullscreen, and a projector or TV-sized display when available.
- Open a saved set and confirm the resource works with teacher-created cards as well as the sample set.
- Check that images do not crop, overflow, or obscure controls.
- If an issue is found after publishing, record it through the feedback or admin workflow and correct the source rather than adding a workaround.
