# Classendo online-safety risk assessment

Owner: CLASSENDO LTD

Assessment date: 5 August 2026
Next scheduled review: 5 August 2027, or earlier after a material community change or serious incident

## Current service scope

Classendo accounts are restricted to adults aged 18 or over and are intended for individual teachers. Teachers can create educational content and may deliberately share lesson sets with other Classendo users. There is currently no direct pupil account offering and no user-to-user private messaging.

## Principal risks and current controls

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Current controls | Further action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Illegal or abusive public lesson content | Medium | High | Community reports, moderation, removal and account restrictions; published Community Guidelines and Online Safety Policy | Review reports weekly and document decisions |
| Copyright infringement | Medium | Medium | Rights warranty in Terms, reporting email and published takedown procedure | Keep a takedown log and repeat-infringer record |
| Child sexual exploitation, grooming or sexual content involving minors | Low given adult-only/no messaging design | Critical | 18+ contractual restriction, no direct pupil accounts, reporting and urgent removal/escalation policy | Reassess before adding messaging, comments or pupil accounts |
| Hate, threats, terrorism or encouragement of self-harm | Low–medium | High | Prohibited-content rules, report route, human moderation and appeal route | Create incident severity and escalation templates |
| Personal information, doxxing or identifiable pupil data | Medium | High | Terms and signup warning prohibit sensitive pupil data; privacy and community reporting routes | Add automated/manual spot checks as public content volume grows |
| Scam links, malware or impersonation | Low–medium | High | Account controls, report route and content/account removal powers | Add link scanning and rate limits when link volume grows |
| Inaccurate or unsuitable AI-generated teaching content | Medium | Medium | Adult teacher review required; AI disclaimer; pupil personal data warning | Test safety filters and monitor provider changes |

## Reporting and response priorities

Critical reports should be preserved, restricted from view and reviewed immediately when discovered. Where there is an imminent threat or a legal reporting obligation, contact the appropriate emergency service, law-enforcement body or specialist reporting organisation. Other reports should be acknowledged and resolved in a reasonable, documented timeframe, with an appeal route where appropriate.

## Residual assessment

The current risk is moderated by adult-only accounts, the educational context and the absence of direct messaging or pupil accounts. Public user-created content still creates meaningful moderation, privacy, copyright and illegal-content risks. The controls above must scale before public-content volume or interactive features materially increase.
